Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Booking

Is the discovery call really free?

Yes, 100%. No hidden fees, no obligation. It's our way of understanding your needs and seeing if we're the right fit to help you on your property journey.

How long is the call?

30 minutes. We respect your time and ensure every minute is valuable, with a structured agenda to cover everything that matters to you.

What if I need to reschedule?

No problem at all. You'll receive a link in your confirmation email to reschedule at any time that works better for you.

Do I need to prepare anything?

It helps to have a rough idea of your budget and goals, but it's not required. Use our checklist above to make the most of your time!

Which timezones do you accommodate?

We accommodate UK, US (EST/PST), Canadian, and West African time zones. We'll find a slot that works for your schedule.

What happens after the call?

You'll receive a summary email with personalised recommendations and clear next steps within 24 hours of your call.
