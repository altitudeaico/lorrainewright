Professional photo of Lorraine Wright MBE

My Story

I've Walked the Path You're Considering

I know exactly what it feels like to dream of owning property in Ghana while living thousands of miles away. The excitement of imagining your own home or investment on Ghanaian soil, mixed with that nagging uncertainty — can I really do this from abroad? Who can I trust? Will my money be safe? I've felt every single one of those emotions because I've been in your shoes.

As a member of the Ghanaian diaspora based in the UK, I made my own property investments in Ghana and experienced first-hand both the incredible opportunities and the real challenges that come with building from afar. I learned which pitfalls to avoid, which questions to ask, and how to ensure every cedi is accounted for. That journey — with all its lessons — became the foundation of Diaspora to Home.

Today, I combine my personal diaspora experience with nearly two decades of professional property expertise across Ghana's most sought-after locations. My dual perspective means I don't just understand the Ghanaian property market — I understand you. Your concerns, your aspirations, and the unique position of investing in a homeland you may visit only once or twice a year. That understanding is what makes Diaspora to Home different.
