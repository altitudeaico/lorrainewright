Your Questions Answered

Common Questions from Diaspora Investors

Everything you need to know before taking your first step

Do I need to visit Ghana to buy property?
No. While visiting is ideal, it's not required. I can arrange virtual viewings, coordinate site visits through trusted representatives, and handle all documentation remotely. Many of my clients complete their entire purchase from the UK, US, or Canada.
How do I know developers are legitimate?
Every developer I work with has been personally vetted over 15 years. I verify their track record, inspect completed projects, review legal documentation, and ensure they have proper registration. You get my "Vetted by Lorraine" guarantee on all properties I recommend.
What's the typical timeline from search to ownership?
With my guidance, most clients complete their purchase in 3-6 months. This includes property search, due diligence, legal review, and final transfer. DIY investors typically take 6-12 months. My Elite service can fast-track this to 2-3 months.
Can I get a mortgage as a diaspora investor?
Yes! I have partnerships with lenders who specialize in diaspora mortgages. You'll need proof of income, a deposit (typically 20-30%), and valid ID. I can guide you through the entire mortgage application process and connect you with the right lenders.
What if something goes wrong after I purchase?
My support doesn't end at purchase. I provide post-purchase guidance including property management connections, Airbnb setup support, and ongoing advice. If issues arise with developers, I leverage my relationships to help resolve them quickly.
