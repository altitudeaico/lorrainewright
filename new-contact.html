Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to invest in Ghana property from abroad?

Yes, absolutely. Ghana has one of the most stable political environments in Africa and a growing economy. With proper due diligence and an experienced partner like Lorraine Wright MBE, investing from abroad is both safe and straightforward. We handle the entire process from legal verification to property management.

What is the minimum investment amount?

Investment opportunities start from under $100,000, with a range of options for different budgets. During your consultation, we'll discuss your financial goals and recommend properties that match your budget and expected returns.

Do I need to visit Ghana before investing?

While we recommend visiting when possible, it's not required. We provide detailed virtual tours, drone footage, and comprehensive documentation for every property. Many of our clients complete their first investment entirely remotely before visiting on their own schedule.

How long does the purchasing process take?

The typical purchasing process takes 4–8 weeks from initial agreement to completion, depending on the type of property and any legal requirements. We guide you through every step, from due diligence and contract negotiation to final transfer of ownership.

What ongoing support do you provide after purchase?

We offer comprehensive post-purchase support including property management, tenant sourcing, rental collection, maintenance coordination, and regular property reports. Our goal is to make your investment truly hands-off while maximising your returns.
