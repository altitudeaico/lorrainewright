FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common Questions About My Services

How much do your services cost?

The discovery call is completely free. For other services, pricing depends on the property value, location, and scope of work. I provide custom quotes based on your specific needs. Contact me for a personalized estimate.

Do I have to visit Ghana?

Not necessarily. Many clients successfully invest remotely with detailed property reports, virtual tours, and video walkthroughs. However, I do recommend visiting at least once before finalizing, and I can coordinate the entire trip for you.

What if I'm not ready to invest yet?

That's perfectly fine! Start with the free discovery call to learn more and get your questions answered. There's no pressure, and I'm happy to guide you through the research phase until you're ready.

Can you help with financing?

While I don't provide financing directly, I can guide you through payment structures, connect you with mortgage providers in Ghana, and advise on payment plans offered by developers. Many properties offer flexible payment options.

How long does the process take?

Typically 3–6 months from initial consultation to property handover, but this varies based on your availability, decision-making pace, and property type (off-plan vs. completed). I work on your timeline.

Do you work with first-time investors?

Absolutely! Most of my clients are first-time Ghana property investors. I specialize in guiding diaspora investors through their first purchase, ensuring you understand every step of the process.

What locations do you cover?

I work across Ghana's major investment hotspots including Accra (East Legon, Airport Residential, Cantonments), Tema, Kumasi, Takoradi, and Cape Coast. If you have a specific area in mind, let's discuss.
