FREQUENTLY ASKED

Quick Answers

Is the call really free?

Yes! The 30-minute discovery call is completely free with no strings attached. It's my way of helping you make an informed decision about Ghana property investment.

What if I'm not ready to invest?

That's perfectly fine! Many people book a call while still in the research phase. I'm here to provide guidance whether you're ready to invest tomorrow or in 12 months.

Can I reschedule if needed?

Absolutely. Life happens! You can reschedule or cancel up to 24 hours before the call using the link in your confirmation email.

Will you pressure me to buy?

Never. This is a discovery call, not a sales pitch. My goal is to understand your needs and provide honest guidance—whether that means working together or pointing you in another direction.

What happens after the call?

I'll send you a summary email with key takeaways, recommended resources, and suggested next steps. You decide if and when you'd like to move forward.

Do you call internationally?

Yes! I regularly speak with diaspora investors in the UK, US, Canada, and beyond. We can connect via Zoom, WhatsApp, or regular phone—whatever works best for you.
