What you now have

You have a complete pipeline that turns a seller's enquiry into a live, vetted property listing on your website — no spreadsheets, no copy-pasting. Sellers submit their property through a branded form and pay the $99 listing fee. Your team reviews each one against a vetting checklist and, with one click, publishes the approved ones. Approved properties appear automatically on your listings page, which lives inside your GoHighLevel site as a single block of code. Buyer enquiries flow straight into your GHL CRM.

Walkthrough

Try the journey, step by step

Pick a role and click through exactly what happens. Nothing here changes anything — it's a guided tour.

Seller · step 1

Open the listing form

You share one link with sellers (on social media, in a DM, or as a button on your site). It opens a clean, branded form titled "List your property".

The form works on a phone — most sellers will fill it in on mobile.
Seller · step 2

Add the property details & photos

They enter the title, location, price, type, and a short description, then upload up to six photos. Required fields are marked, so nothing important gets missed.

  • Title, location, type, name and email are required.
  • Photos upload straight into secure storage.
  • A consent box must be ticked before they can submit.
Seller · step 3

Submit

They press submit. Behind the scenes the submission saves to your database, and GoHighLevel emails you a new-lead alert and emails them a confirmation.

Seller · step 4

Pay the $99 listing fee

The success screen shows a "Pay $99" button and a clear "what happens next". They pay through your FastPayDirect link.

If they pay later, your team simply marks the listing paid once the payment lands.
Seller · step 5

They wait for approval

That's the seller's part done. The property is now in your queue, waiting for your team to review it. Nothing goes public until you approve it.

Team · step 1

Log in to the workspace

Open the workspace link and sign in with your shared team login. Personal seller details never leave this logged-in screen.

Team · step 2

Open a submission

New submissions appear in the queue on the left. Click one to see the seller's details, their photos, and the evidence they provided.

Team · step 3

Work the vetting checklist

Go through the checks (ownership, documents, location, and so on). As you mark them, the system suggests a verdict — Verified, Seller's Listing, Reject, or Needs more info.

Red-line checks are flagged clearly, so a property can't be marked "Verified" unless the essentials are met.
Team · step 4

Confirm payment

Each submission shows a Paid / Unpaid pill. Once you've confirmed the $99 arrived in GHL, click Mark as paid. If you try to approve an unpaid one, you'll get a gentle warning first.

Team · step 5

Publish with one click

Click Approve as Verified or List as Seller's Listing. The property is published to your public site immediately — no code, no spreadsheet.

Team · step 6

Manage it anytime

The Listings tab lets you edit the title, price or location, mark a property sold, unpublish it, or remove it. To clear an entry entirely, use Delete this submission — it removes the submission and its listing together.

Setup · step 1

Copy the embed code

In the workspace, open the Listings tab. At the top is a "Website embed code" box — click Copy embed code. This always copies the current, correct version.

Setup · step 2

Open your GHL page

In GoHighLevel, open the page where you want the property grid to appear, in the page builder.

Setup · step 3

Add a Custom HTML element

Drag in a Custom HTML element (sometimes called "Code" or "Custom Code / HTML"). This is the one rule that matters: it must be an HTML/Code block, not a text block.

If the styling ever looks off, it's almost always because the code got pasted into a text block. Re-paste into a Custom HTML element.
Setup · step 4

Paste & save

Paste the copied code into that element and save. Preview the page — the property grid appears and loads your live listings.

Setup · step 5

You're done — it stays current

You only paste it once. Whenever you publish, edit, mark sold, or unpublish a listing in the workspace, the page updates itself. No need to touch GHL again.

GHL setup & details

The GoHighLevel side, explained

The parts that connect to your CRM — what's already wired up, and what to keep in mind.

  1. Copy the embed code from the workspace (Listings tab → Copy embed code).
  2. In GHL, drag a Custom HTML / Code element onto the page.
  3. Paste the code in and save.

The font loads in a way that survives GHL's editor, and all styling is self-contained, so it won't clash with your template. It must go in a Code/HTML block — not a text block.

When a seller submits, the system pings a GHL Inbound Webhook, which triggers your "Property Listing Submission" workflow. That workflow:

  • Creates or updates the seller as a contact.
  • Emails your team a new-lead alert.
  • Emails the seller a confirmation.

This is built and confirmed working. If emails ever stop arriving, check the sending domain / from-address in GHL first.

The seller pays through your FastPayDirect link on the form's success screen. Reconciliation is manual by design:

  1. Confirm the $99 landed (in FastPayDirect / GHL).
  2. In the workspace, click Mark as paid on that submission.

A built-in warning stops a property being approved while it still shows unpaid, so nothing slips through.

Every listing has a "Find out more / request a viewing" form. When a buyer submits it, the enquiry posts straight into your GHL workflow as a lead — tagged with the property they asked about.

Before you go public: the enquiry form ships in a safe "demo" mode so test enquiries don't hit your CRM. When you're ready for real buyer leads, we switch it to live.

  • Schedule a Tour → connect the button to your GHL booking calendar so buyers can book viewings.
  • Seller decision emails → automatically tell a seller when they're approved or need to send more documents.

Both are straightforward to add when you want them.
