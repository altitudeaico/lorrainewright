## 🎨 CONTENT TONE & MESSAGING

### **Voice Principles:**
1. **Authentic, not corporate** - "I've been scammed so you don't have to be"
2. **Educational, not sales-y** - Give value first
3. **Confident, not arrogant** - Credibility without ego
4. **Warm, not distant** - Fellow diaspora investor, not distant expert
5. **Specific, not vague** - "£5,999" not "competitive pricing"

### **Power Words to Use:**
- Legacy | Generational wealth | Vetted | Verified | Insider access
- Peace of mind | Transparent | Proven | Protected | Guided

### **Avoid:**
- "Premium" | "Luxury" | "Exclusive" (overused in real estate)
- Generic real estate jargon
- Overpromising returns
